STUDENT HUB PORTAL

About Us

  • StudentHub is a simple web portal developed for students.

  • It provides easy access to academic information, campus events, student profiles, and communication with the college.

OUR MISSION

  • Make student services easy to access.
  • Improve communication between students and the college.
  • Provide one place for important information.

FEATURES

  • Student Registration.
  • Secure Login
  • Student Dashboard.
  • Event Updates.
  • Profile Management.
  • Feedback System.
  • Frequently Asked Questions.
