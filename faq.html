Frequantly Asked Question

Q1. What is Student Hub Portal?
A. It is an online platform for students to access academic services.

Q2. How do I create an account?
A. Click the Register page, fill in the details, and submit the form.

Q3. How can I view my timetable?
A. Log in and open Dashboard → Timetable.

Q4. How do I register for college events?
A. Go to the Events page and click the Register button.

Q5. Can I update my profile?
A. Yes, open the Profile page and click Edit Profile.

Q6. How can I contact Student Hub support?
A. Visit the Contact Us page and submit your query.
